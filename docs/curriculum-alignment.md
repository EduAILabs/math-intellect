# Curriculum Alignment

## Math Intellect – AI-Assisted Mathematics Learning Platform

**Project Lead:** Ngwe Htoon (Howard)  
**Document status:** Curriculum-reference framework for the current MVP; not evidence of official Cambridge affiliation, certification, or complete syllabus coverage.

## Educational Scope

Math Intellect is designed with reference to **Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (0580)** and **Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606)**. The current MVP and its 51-case validation should not be interpreted as comprehensive, independently demonstrated coverage of every topic, paper type, or syllabus objective in either qualification. Expanded curriculum support and broader controlled validation are planned.

The syllabuses inform how the workflow interprets topics, chooses an appropriate depth of explanation, presents mathematical notation, and reviews assessment-oriented output. This is an independently developed project; Math Intellect is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official marking service of Cambridge University Press & Assessment**.

## Curriculum Reference Framework

| Reference dimension | Intended handling in the workflow |
|---|---|
| Syllabus and topic | Identify the mathematical area and relevant syllabus context when the source question provides sufficient information. |
| Expected level and method | Select reasoning and mathematical techniques appropriate to the stated question. |
| Assessment objectives | Consider AO1 and AO2 when describing the mathematical demands of a question. |
| Calculator context | Respect a stated calculator or non-calculator requirement; do not assume one where the question does not specify it. |
| Communication | Show relevant formulas, intermediate working, units, notation, and final answers clearly. |
| Assessment guidance | Provide examiner-inspired guidance rather than reproducing or claiming to issue an official mark scheme. |

## Assessment Objectives

The reference framework uses the two assessment-objective categories described in the project report:

- **AO1 — Knowledge and understanding of mathematical techniques:** recall and application of knowledge, routine procedures, calculation, notation, and handling mathematical information.
- **AO2 — Analyse, interpret and communicate mathematically:** selection of approaches, interpretation, connections, logical conclusions, and communication of methods and results.

Question-level AO labels are **AI-assisted classifications**, not a certification that Cambridge has assigned an identical label or mark allocation to the submitted question. The system should avoid asserting an objective where the source or available evidence is ambiguous.

## Application Across the Pipeline

| Stage | Curriculum-related responsibility |
|---|---|
| Question Analyzer | Identify syllabus context, topic, givens, question demand, and any explicit calculator restrictions. |
| Solver | Apply appropriate methods, make working visible, and respect relevant contextual constraints. |
| Examiner | Produce assessment-oriented commentary on demonstrated working and answers. |
| Moderator | Check whether assessment and solution refer to the same interpreted question. |
| Quality Checker | Review mathematical correctness, completeness, and presentation. |
| Formatter | Retain relevant mathematical notation and present the reviewed response clearly through LINE. |

These are intended stage responsibilities. An AI-generated classification or review may be wrong, especially when the input image contains important diagram information.

## Assessment and Marking Boundary

Examiner-inspired guidance is intended to help a learner understand which methods and reasoning are relevant. It must not be labelled an official Cambridge mark scheme, certified grading result, or guarantee of the marks that an examiner would award. Where assessment references are used for internal evaluation, reviewers should consult the relevant authorized source materials and record the reference without publicly reproducing copyrighted paper or mark-scheme content without permission.

## Current Limitations and Validation

The existing technical validation comprises 51 test cases and identifies visual/diagram interpretation as a major limitation. Consequently, curriculum-fit and assessment-alignment scores cannot establish universal syllabus compliance or correctness across all question formats. Any claim of increased student learning or examination performance requires a separate educational study.
